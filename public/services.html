We Offer:

1. Personal Training

One-on-one coaching, fully customized to your goals. Work closely with our certified personal trainers who will create a workout and nutrition plan tailored just for you. Each session is goal-focused, whether it's weight loss, muscle gain, or improving strength and endurance.

  • Schedule: Flexible timings between 6 AM - 8 PM (Mon-Sat)
  • Session Duration: 60 minutes
  • Trainer: Choose your preferred male/female trainer
  • Progress Tracking: Weekly assessments included
  • Pricing:
    • NRs. 1,500/session
    • NRs. 12,000/month (3 sessions/week)
    • NRs. 20,000/month (5 sessions/week)

Best for individuals who want a focused, efficient, and guided transformation.

2. Strength Training

Progressive training to help you build real strength and muscle. Whether you're a beginner or intermediate lifter, this structured plan helps you master core lifts, improve posture, and build a strong physique.

  • Includes: Access to strength area + program template + monthly consultation
  • Equipment: Barbells, dumbbells, kettlebells, resistance machines
  • Open Gym Hours: 6 AM - 9 PM (Mon-Sat)
  • Trainers Available: For form correction and assistance
  • Pricing:
    • NRs. 2,000/month (gym access only)
    • NRs. 3,500/month (with monthly consultation)

Ideal for gym-goers looking to train independently with expert structure.

3. Group Fitness Classes

Fun, energetic classes designed to keep you motivated. Train in a lively group setting with structured workouts led by expert instructors. Perfect for those who enjoy community support and high energy.

  • Class Types: HIIT, Zumba, Circuit Training, Functional Strength
  • Schedule: Fixed batch timings (Morning: 6-7 AM, Evening: 6-7 PM)
  • Class Size: Max 12 people per class
  • Trainers: Certified group instructors for each session
  • Pricing:
    • NRs. 1,200/week (3 classes/week)
    • NRs. 3,000/month (Unlimited classes)
    • Drop-in: NRs. 500/class

Best for individuals who want a focused, efficient, and guided transformation.
